Shared apostolic teaching (the “Rule of Faith”)

The Novatian Schism (c. 251 AD) — over whether Christians who had lapsed under persecution could be restored. Novatian formed a stricter separate group. This was treated as a schism.

3. What Did “Orthodox” Mean Before It Became a Denominational Label?
This is important.
Before it became the name of a denomination (“Eastern Orthodox Church”), the word “orthodox” was simply an adjective meaning:

“Right belief” or
“Right worship / right glory”

It described teaching and practice that aligned with the apostles.

Early writers defended what they called the apostolic tradition against groups they saw as deviating (especially Gnostics and Marcionites).
